Before You Donate

A little preparation can make the process easier and safer.

  • Eat a healthy meal before donating.
  • Drink enough water.
  • Get proper rest.
  • Bring identification if required.
  • Tell the staff about any illness or medicines you take.

If you are feeling unwell or taking certain medicines, consult the blood bank staff before donating.

◷

When Should You Wait?

You may need to postpone donation if you:

  • Have a fever or feel unwell.
  • Have a recent infection or surgery.
  • Are taking certain medicines.
♡

After Donation

Take care of yourself after donating.

  • Rest for a short time.
  • Have refreshments as advised.
  • Drink enough fluids.
  • Follow the staff's instructions.

Final eligibility depends on medical screening and current blood bank guidelines.
